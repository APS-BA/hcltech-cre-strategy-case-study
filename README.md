## The problem

How much office space will the company need in India over the next three years,
where, and should any sites be consolidated or added?

## What I did

| Workstream | Scope | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Peer benchmarking** | 6 IT-services peers, 302 facilities, 27 cities | Public-domain facility data; space per seat, seats per employee, ownership mix |
| **Demand projection** | 9 cities × 9 lines of business, Apr 2026–Mar 2029 | Two independent methods (weighted average and moving average) for headcount (FTE) and return-to-office demand; 21 projection charts |
| **Consolidation model** | One city's multi-site footprint | 5-category incremental cost model with rent-sensitivity break-even |
| **Recommendation** | Portfolio strategy | The analysis changed direction on a proposed expansion plan |

## Outcome

The consolidation option came out financially unfavourable, and the recommendation
shifted future capacity investment toward Tier-2 cities and GIFT City.
